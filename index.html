Safeguards this flow enforces
  • Fails closed. An unreviewed clip is never published. Nothing goes out unchecked, even if that means a child gets no reel this week.
  • No child name leaves the system. The notification says "your child’s reel", never a name, number or thumbnail. The reel opens only behind that family’s login.
  • A coach sees one roster. Review is scoped to their own team, so resolving a collision never exposes another club’s children.
  • Every decision is attributed. Confirm, reassign and discard are written to an audit trail with who and when, because reassignment moves a child’s footage.
  • Discarded footage is deleted, not orphaned. A clip matching no roster player is queued for deletion rather than sitting unlinked in storage.
  • Page hardening. Content Security Policy blocks all external and inline scripts, no referrer is sent, all rendered values are escaped, and the page loads no third party code, fonts or trackers.
